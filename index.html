Privacy Policy for Weft

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Contact: weftlylabs@gmail.com

Weft (“we,” “our,” or “the Extension”) is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab with a personal dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use Weft.

Summary: Weft works fully without an account, and your dashboard data stays on your device by default. If you choose to sign in, an optional account lets your dashboard sync across your devices through our backend (Supabase); see §3. We also send a small anonymous usage signal by default (see §4), which you can turn off. Optional features may send limited data to third-party services only when you use those features (see below).

1. Information we do not collect

Weft does not:

  • Require an account or sign-up to use the extension
  • Sell your data
  • Use your data for advertising or creditworthiness
  • Tie the anonymous usage signal in §4 to your identity or account

We do not operate servers that store your dashboard content unless you turn on the optional account and sync feature described in §3.

2. Data stored on your device (default)

By default, all dashboard data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local), including for example:

  • Tasks, habits, jobs, links, sticky notes, and entertainment lists
  • Spaces, widget layout, wallpaper, theme, and settings
  • Reminders and notification preferences
  • Any API keys or access tokens you enter for optional integrations (see §6)

This data remains on your device. We cannot access it remotely.

You can export or import backups manually from Settings → Data & Backup.

3. Optional Weft account and sync

If you choose to sign in (Settings → Account), currently via Google:

  • We collect your email address, name, and avatar image from Google, to identify your account.
  • Signing in does not turn sync on by itself; sync is a separate toggle, off until you choose it, with per-group switches (Tasks, Habits, Links & shortcuts, Sticky notes, Jobs, Entertainment, Spaces & widgets, Preferences) so you control exactly what leaves your device. Reminders never sync; they stay per device.
  • Data for the groups you enable is stored on our backend (Supabase) so it can reach your other signed-in devices. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access-controlled so only your signed-in account can read or write it.
  • Deleting an item you sync (a task, a link, a note, etc.) does not erase it from our backend immediately. It is kept, recoverable, for up to 15 days in case the deletion was a mistake, then automatically and permanently cleared.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account) permanently removes your synced data and profile from our backend. Your local, on-device dashboard is not affected.
  • This feature is opt-in and off by default. Weft’s core functionality never requires an account.

4. Anonymous usage signal

Once a day per install, Weft sends a small anonymous signal: which widgets you have enabled, whether you use the Spaces feature, and the extension version. This is not tied to your account, your email, or any other identifier that could connect it back to you — it uses a random id generated for your install, stored only on your device.

We use this to understand which parts of Weft are actually used, not to profile individuals. You can turn this off any time in Settings → Account.

5. Voluntary feedback (in-app)

If you submit a bug report or widget request from Settings, you may optionally include:

  • A description of the issue or request
  • An optional contact email

That information is sent to our team via Discord (a third-party service) only when you click Send. We use it solely to respond to or triage your feedback. Do not include sensitive personal data in feedback messages.

7. Embedded Website widget

The optional Embedded Website widget renders a third-party page of your choosing inside a resizable panel on your dashboard, using a sandboxed iframe. To allow sites that normally block framing, the extension removes the X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy response headers, using declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess, but only for the exact hostnames you add to an embed widget and only for iframe (sub-frame) requests. Weft does not read, log, or transmit the contents of embedded pages. The embedded site runs in its own origin as ordinary web content.

8. Tab Audio widget

The Tab Audio widget uses a content script to read and adjust audio levels in browser tabs you interact with. Audio processing happens locally in your browser. Weft does not record, transmit, or store your audio.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: Removed when you uninstall the extension or clear extension data in Chrome.
  • Synced account data: Kept on our backend while your account exists and sync is on; a deleted item is kept up to 15 days for recovery before being permanently cleared. Deleting your account removes it entirely.
  • Anonymous usage signal: One row per install per day; not linked to an account, so there is nothing account-specific to delete on request.
  • Feedback: Retained in our Discord workspace only as long as needed for support and product planning.

11. Security

Because your data is stored locally (and optionally synced to our backend if you sign in), its security depends on your device, browser, and account protections. Use device lock, a strong Google password, and Chrome’s built-in security features.

12. Children

Weft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
