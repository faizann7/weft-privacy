Privacy Policy for Weft

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Contact: weftlylabs@gmail.com

Weft (“we,” “our,” or “the Extension”) is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab with a personal dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use Weft. By using Weft, you agree to this policy.

Summary: Weft works fully without an account, and your dashboard data stays on your device by default. An account, cloud sync and a paid plan are optional. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Optional features contact third-party services only when you use them.

1. Data stored on your device

By default, everything you add to Weft (tasks, habits, links, notes, layouts, settings, wallpapers and similar) is stored locally in your browser using Chrome’s extension storage. We cannot access it remotely. Any keys or tokens you enter for optional integrations are stored there too. You can export and import backups from Settings.

2. Information we collect

We collect information only in the situations below.

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in (currently with Google), we receive your email address, name and profile picture to identify your account.
  • Synced content. If you turn on sync, the dashboard groups you choose, including wallpaper photos you upload, are stored on our backend so they can reach your other devices. Sync is off until you turn it on.
  • Purchase information. If you buy Weft Pro, payment is handled by our payment provider, which acts as merchant of record. We do not receive or store your card or billing details. We receive your email, plan and subscription status so Pro can be unlocked on your account.
  • Usage signal. Once a day, Weft sends a small pseudonymous signal (which widgets are enabled and the extension version) tied to a random identifier generated on your device, not to your account or contact details. It never includes the content of your dashboard. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Feedback. If you send a bug report or request from Settings, we receive what you submit, including an email address if you add one.
  • Website analytics. Our marketing website uses aggregated, cookie-free traffic and performance measurement.

Weft does not collect or track your browsing history. It does not require an account to use, and we do not use your data to profile you.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate Weft, sync and restore your data, process subscriptions and manage access to paid features, understand which features are used, respond to feedback, keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Optional features and third parties

Some widgets and integrations work by contacting a third-party service, for example prayer times, sports scores, movie and show information, stock quotes, favicons, online wallpapers, Spotify, GitHub and Twitch. This happens only when you use that feature, and we send only what it needs to work, such as a city name, a search term, or a ticker symbol. We do not send your dashboard to these services.

Credentials you provide for an integration, such as an API key or a sign-in token, are stored on your device and sent only to that provider. For features that use your location, location is read only when you ask for it, once per request.

We also rely on service providers to run Weft, such as backend hosting and storage, payment and subscription management, website hosting and analytics, and the messaging service that receives feedback. They may use information only to provide their services to us.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with the service providers described above, with legal or regulatory authorities where required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public, and with a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of all or part of the service.

6. Browser permissions

Weft asks for browser permissions only to provide features you use, for example reading your clipboard for the optional save-link shortcut, tab access for the Tab Audio widget, and notifications for reminders. The optional Embedded Website widget removes framing restrictions only for the sites you add to it, so they can display inside your dashboard. Weft does not read or transmit the content of embedded pages or your audio.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall Weft or clear its data. Synced data is kept on our backend while your account exists, including after sync is turned off or a paid plan ends, so you can restore it later. Items you delete are kept for a short recovery period, then permanently removed. You can delete your account in Settings, which permanently removes your synced data, uploaded wallpaper photos and profile from our backend. Your local dashboard is not affected. Copies may remain briefly in routine backups of our hosting provider after deletion. Payment records are held by our payment provider under its own terms.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, encryption of stored data by our hosting provider, and access controls. Because your data is stored locally and, if you choose, synced to our backend, its security also depends on your device, browser and account protections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract (to provide Weft), your consent (for optional features you turn on), our legitimate interests (such as keeping Weft secure and working), and compliance with legal obligations.

10. International data transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can do much of this in Settings. Otherwise, contact us at weftlylabs@gmail.com.

12. Children

Weft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Weft after an update means you accept the revised policy.
